## 🎨 Design Specifications

### **Image Dimensions:**
- **Hero Video Thumbnail**: 1920x1080px (16:9)
- **Product Mockups**: 800x600px (4:3)
- **Testimonial Avatars**: 100x100px (1:1)
- **Category Previews**: 400x300px (4:3)
- **Logo**: 300x100px (3:1 ratio)
- **Icons**: 64x64px (1:1, SVG preferred)

### **File Formats:**
- **Photos**: JPG (optimized, 80% quality)
- **Graphics with transparency**: PNG
- **Icons**: SVG (scalable)
- **Videos**: MP4 (H.264, web-optimized)

### **Color Scheme:**
- **Primary**: #6366f1 (Indigo)
- **Secondary**: #22d3ee (Cyan)
- **Accent**: #10b981 (Emerald)
- **Background**: Dark theme (#0f172a, #1e293b)
